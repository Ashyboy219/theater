# THEATER — How to Play (the 4X layer)

THEATER plays like Red Alert at its core — build a base, harvest, build an army, fight — but layers a
4X-style progression on top. This is the short guide to *using* that depth. (For what each unit is, hover
its tooltip; for the real-world systems behind them, see the [Codex](theater-codex.md).)

## The 60-second version

1. Pick a **faction** — each plays differently (precision air, heavy armor, drone mass, EW, layered standoff…).
2. Build the normal RA economy (Refinery + Harvesters) and army.
3. Build a **Theater Command (THCOM)** → unlocks the **Command** sidebar tab (your strategy menu) and projects
   a Command & Control aura (your nearby units hit harder).
4. Build a **Research Lab (RLAB)** → unlocks the **research tree** and **age advancement** in that same tab.
5. Climb: **research upgrades → advance ages → build Alloy Extractors → bank alloys → field apex units.**

## The strategic tab (your "Command" queue)

Everything 4X is bought here, top to bottom in roughly the order you'll want it:

- **Capabilities** — buy-once *Fire Control* (army-wide targeting focus) and *Formations*.
- **Doctrine** — a one-time, mutually-exclusive fork for your faction (e.g. Armored Breakthrough vs Artillery
  Saturation). Defines your mid-game identity. Choose deliberately — you can't switch.
- **Research Tier 1** — accumulating army-wide upgrades (ballistics, armor, optics, propulsion, small arms,
  logistics) **plus the first decision fork:** *Mass* (cheaper, frailer) **vs** *Precision* (stronger, pricier).
- **Ages** — *Information → Autonomous → Orbital*. Each is a real cost+time investment that stacks a cumulative
  army-wide power bump **and** gates the next tier. This is your main lever for a longer, escalating game.
- **Research Tier 2** (needs Information Age) — net-centric range, reactive armor, autoloaders, avionics, naval.
- **Economic & Posture forks** (needs Information Age) — two more mutually-exclusive decisions:
  - *War Economy* (cheaper units, weaker income) **vs** *Industrial Base* (richer income, pricier units).
  - *Maneuver* (faster, farther-seeing mobile units) **vs** *Fortress* (tougher, longer-range static defenses).
- **Economy Network** — boosts all your income buildings.

## The economy: cash AND alloys

- **Cash** (from ore, as usual) builds everything.
- **Alloys** are a **second, counted resource** — your strategic-mineral stockpile, shown in the sidebar.
  Build **Alloy Extractors** to bank alloys over time (build several — expansion pays). The **apex tier
  requires a stockpile of alloys and spends them on build**, so you can't reach the top without committing
  to strategic-resource infrastructure.
- Optional economy buildings: the **Bank** (pure stackable income — out-economy your opponent) and the
  **Fusion Reactor** (high-output power for the hungry late game).

## The payoff: apex units

Once you reach the **Orbital Age** and have banked enough **alloys**, you can field the gargantuan apex units —
double-size, faction-gated, BuildLimit 2 each: the **Leviathan** (naval dreadnought), **Citadel** (land
fortress), **Archangel** (heavy gunship), and **Tempest** (rocket artillery). They are the "look at this HUGE
thing" reward for climbing the whole ladder.

## Key decisions that make games diverge

- Your **faction** and its **doctrine fork** — national identity.
- **Mass vs Precision** — quantity or quality.
- **War Economy vs Industrial Base** — guns now, or out-economy later.
- **Maneuver vs Fortress** — mobile aggression, or an entrenched kill-zone.
- **How fast to climb ages** vs spend on army now — tempo.
- **Where to fight** — near your THCOM for the C2 bonus (defend), or push out (you give it up).

Two games with different choices play very differently — that's the point.

## Quick tips

- Don't neglect **alloys** if you want apex units — start Extractors early; one is slow, several compound.
- The **Theater Command** is a high-value target: losing it drops your Command queue, paradrop, *and* the C2
  aura. Defend it; hunt the enemy's.
- The **ages** buff *everyone* who advances — falling behind on age is falling behind on raw power.
- Research forks are **permanent** — commit to a coherent build rather than splitting.
