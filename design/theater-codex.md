# THEATER Codex — the real world behind the game

One of THEATER's founding goals is to be quietly *educational*: almost every unit is modeled on a real
contemporary military system, and the strategic-resource economy maps onto real critical-minerals geopolitics.
This Codex is the reference — accurate, public-knowledge facts about what the in-game pieces are based on.
(All figures are approximate and from open sources; this is a game, not a defense brief.)

---

## The strategic resource: critical minerals & rare earths

THEATER's `alloys` resource — required to reach the apex tier — is a stand-in for the **critical minerals**
that modern militaries actually depend on.

- **Rare-earth elements (REEs)** are a set of 17 metals (the lanthanides plus scandium and yttrium). Despite
  the name they aren't geologically rare, but economically viable, *refinable* deposits are uncommon.
- They are indispensable to modern defense and high tech: **neodymium** and **dysprosium** go into the
  permanent magnets in jet actuators, missile fins, precision-guided munitions, radar, electric motors and
  EVs; **yttrium/europium** into displays and lasers; **samarium-cobalt** magnets tolerate high heat.
- The supply chain is **highly concentrated**: a single country accounts for a large majority of global
  rare-earth *refining* (roughly ~85–90% historically), which is why "who controls the alloys" is a real
  strategic question — and why, in THEATER, you can't field the largest platforms without securing extractors.
- Other contested critical minerals include **lithium, cobalt, nickel, graphite, gallium and germanium** —
  inputs to batteries, semiconductors and optoelectronics, several of which have been subject to real export
  controls in recent years.

The design point: in a modern war, **economic and resource control is upstream of military power** — which is
exactly the 4X loop THEATER models (expand → secure alloys → unlock the apex).

## The units → the real systems

### Federation (USA)
- **B-21 (flagship)** → **B-21 Raider**, the U.S. Air Force's next-generation stealth bomber (Northrop
  Grumman), publicly unveiled in December 2022 and intended to succeed the B-2 Spirit.
- **AMX (flagship)** → **AbramsX**, General Dynamics' next-gen Abrams technology demonstrator (shown 2022) —
  lighter, hybrid-electric drive, reduced crew.
- **Burke** → **Arleigh Burke-class destroyer**, the U.S. Navy's Aegis-equipped guided-missile destroyer and
  one of the most-produced large surface combatants in the world.
- **Reaper / Specter / Raptor** → U.S. ISR/strike drones and stealth fighters (MQ-9, F-22/F-35 lineage).

### Northern Union (Russia)
- **Armata / Molot** → **T-14 Armata**, Russia's next-generation MBT, notable for its **unmanned turret** and
  crew housed in an armored capsule.
- **Tunguska** → **2K22 Tunguska / Pantsir**-class self-propelled anti-aircraft systems combining guns + SAMs.
- **Akula** → **Akula-class** (Project 971) nuclear attack submarine.
- **BMPT** → **BMPT "Terminator"**, a tank-support fighting vehicle built to screen armor against infantry/AT.

### Continental Bloc (China)
- **GJ-11** → **GJ-11 "Sharp Sword"**, a Chinese flying-wing stealth combat drone.
- **Wing Loong** → **Wing Loong** (Chengdu) MALE strike/reconnaissance drone family.
- **Houbei** → **Type 022 Houbei**, a stealthy catamaran missile fast-attack craft built for littoral
  anti-ship missile swarms.

### Anatolian Alliance (Turkey)
- **Akinci** → **Bayraktar Akıncı**, Baykar's high-altitude long-endurance armed UCAV.
- **Bayrak** → **Bayraktar TB2**, the widely-exported tactical strike drone.
- **Koral** → **KORAL**, a Turkish land-based electronic-warfare (radar jamming) system.

### Isles Coalition (UK)
- **Astute** → **Astute-class** nuclear attack submarine, the Royal Navy's quietest, with advanced sonar.
- **Pathfinder / Raider** → UK special-forces lineage (Pathfinders / SAS).

### Subcontinent Federation (India)
- **Agni (flagship)** → **Agni** missile family, India's medium-to-intercontinental-range ballistic missiles.
- **Kolkata** → **Kolkata-class** guided-missile destroyer.
- **Tejas** → **HAL Tejas**, India's indigenous light multirole fighter.
- **Akash** → **Akash**, India's medium-range mobile surface-to-air missile system.
- **NAMICA** → **NAMICA** (Nag Missile Carrier), an ATGM tank-destroyer.

### Eastern Maritime Pact (Japan) & Rhine Compact (Germany)
- **Kunai** → JGSDF anti-tank infantry (Type-79/87 lineage). **Aegis** → Aegis Ashore / SAM point defense.
- **Loewe / Pioneer** → German armored + combat-engineer doctrine (Leopard lineage).

### The apex "spectacle" units
The four gargantuan units are deliberately *near-future / conceptual* rather than fielded systems — the
**Leviathan** (an "arsenal ship" — a heavily-armed missile/gun platform, a concept the U.S. Navy has studied),
the **Citadel** (a land-battleship, in the lineage of paper projects like the Ratte), the **Archangel**
(a heavy gunship / "flying fortress" in the AC-130 tradition), and the **Tempest** (a strategic MLRS scaled
up). They are the game's "what if you took it to the limit" payoff.
